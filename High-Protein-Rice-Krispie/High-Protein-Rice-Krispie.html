High-Protein-Rice-Krispie

Homepage

Description

These high-protein Rice Krispie treats are an easy way to add a little extra nutrition to a nostalgic favorite. Protein powder adds a satisfying boost without changing the crispy, gooey texture you love, while a pinch of salt brings out the sweet, buttery flavor.

Ingredients

  • 6 tablespoons butter
  • 6 cups mini marshmallows
  • 3 scoops vanilla protein powder
  • 1 pinch kosher salt
  • 6 cups crispy rice cereal

Steps

  1. Lightly grease an 11x7-inch or 9x13-inch baking dish (a smaller dish will yield a thicker treat). Heat butter in a large Dutch oven over medium heat until it melts. Add marshmallows and stir until melted while gradually adding in protein powder and salt.

  2. Stir in cereal and remove from heat. Working quickly, transfer mixture to the prepared dish. Lightly coat hands or a rubber spatula with cooking spray and press mixture evenly into the dish.

  3. Let cool completely at room temperature, at least 1 hour, before cutting into squares and serving.
